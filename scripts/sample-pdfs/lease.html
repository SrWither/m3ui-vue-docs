Residential Lease Agreement

This agreement is made on 1 November 2026 between Harbourview Properties ("the Landlord") and Jonah Reyes ("the Tenant") for the apartment at 21 Willow Street, Flat 4B ("the Premises").

1. Term

The lease begins on 1 December 2026 and ends on 30 November 2027. Either party may end it at the close of the term with sixty days' written notice; without notice, it continues month to month on the same terms.

2. Rent and deposit

Monthly rent is $1,480, due on the first day of each month by bank transfer. A security deposit of $1,480 is held for the length of the tenancy and returned within thirty days of the end of the lease, less the cost of any damage beyond normal wear and tear, itemised in writing.

3. Use of the premises

The Premises are for residential use by the Tenant and up to one additional occupant. Container plants on the balcony are welcome, provided pots stand in drip trays and are kept inside the railing.

4. Maintenance and repairs

The Landlord keeps the structure, plumbing, heating and appliances in good repair and responds to urgent requests within forty-eight hours. The Tenant keeps the Premises clean, reports problems promptly and does not make alterations without written consent.

5. Pets

One cat or one small dog is permitted with an additional deposit of $300, refundable on the same terms as the security deposit.

6. Entry

The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspections or repairs with twenty-four hours' notice, or at any time in an emergency.
